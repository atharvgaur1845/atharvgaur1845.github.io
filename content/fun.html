  Personal Interests

Beyond my academic and research pursuits, I am deeply interested in various domains that complement my technical work:

Technology & Innovation
I am fascinated by emerging technologies and their potential to solve real-world problems. Whether it's advancements in AI, robotics, or IoT, I enjoy exploring how cutting-edge research translates into practical applications. This curiosity drives my engagement with open-source projects and technical communities.

Problem-Solving & Optimization
I enjoy working on challenging algorithmic problems and optimization puzzles. The satisfaction of finding elegant solutions to complex problems is a major motivator in my research work. This extends from academic problems to real-world engineering challenges.

Science Communication
I believe in making complex technical concepts accessible to broader audiences. I enjoy explaining technical ideas and sharing knowledge with peers and mentees. Clear communication of research findings is an integral part of my work.

Creative Technologies
The intersection of art and technology fascinates me. From neural style transfer to generative models, I find it inspiring how machine learning can be applied to creative endeavors while maintaining technical rigor.

  Extracurricular Activities

Robotics Competitions
I actively participate in robotics competitions including the European Rover Challenge (ERC). These experiences provide invaluable hands-on learning opportunities in systems integration, time management, and teamwork. Working on CRISS Robotics projects has been instrumental in developing practical embedded systems skills.

IEEE Student Chapter Leadership
As part of the IEEE BITS Pilani Technical Division, I contribute to organizing technical workshops, mentoring younger students, and leading embedded systems projects. This role allows me to give back to the community and help others develop their technical skills.

Technical Community Engagement
I actively engage with technical communities through GitHub contributions, participating in discussion forums, and sharing knowledge. Building strong connections in the tech community is important for continuous learning and collaboration.

Continuous Learning
I regularly explore new courses, tutorials, and research papers to stay current with the rapidly evolving field of AI and robotics. Whether through online platforms or academic resources, I am committed to expanding my knowledge base.
