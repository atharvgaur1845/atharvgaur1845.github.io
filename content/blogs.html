  Primary Research Interests

Computer Vision & Deep Learning
My primary research focus lies in advancing computer vision through deep learning techniques. I am particularly interested in developing robust models for real-world applications, including deepfake detection, semantic segmentation, and style transfer. My work emphasizes reproducibility, comprehensive evaluation, and practical implementation.

Robotics & Autonomous Systems
I am passionate about developing autonomous systems that can perceive, plan, and act in complex environments. My robotics research spans from SLAM algorithms and path planning to real-time embedded control systems. Current work includes autonomous navigation for lunar rovers and autonomous maze-solving robots.

Reinforcement Learning & Multi-Agent Systems
I am exploring how reinforcement learning can be applied to multi-agent scenarios with safety constraints. My research with the POSTMAN API Lab focuses on neuro-symbolic approaches for ensuring constraint satisfaction in dynamic environments without requiring model retraining.

Mechanistic Interpretability & Explainable AI
Understanding how neural networks make decisions is crucial for trustworthy AI systems. I am developing tools and methodologies for mechanistic interpretability, particularly for transformer architectures. This includes attention analysis, probing techniques, and visualization methods.

Time-Series Analysis for Industrial Applications
Working with Prof. Pradeep Kundu at IIT Indore, I am developing Transformer-based approaches for real-time monitoring of industrial IoT sensor data. This involves handling non-stationary time-series and designing attention mechanisms specifically suited to such complex temporal patterns.

Embedded Systems & Edge Computing
I am interested in the intersection of AI and embedded systems, where models must run efficiently on resource-constrained devices. My work with IEEE BITS Pilani on microcontroller-based robotic systems exemplifies this intersection.

  Research Methodology & Philosophy

My approach to research is grounded in rigorous experimental design and reproducibility:

  • Reproducibility First: All experiments are designed with careful documentation of hyperparameters, data splits, and random seeds to ensure reproducible results.
  • Comprehensive Evaluation: I employ multiple evaluation metrics appropriate to each task, avoiding overfitting to a single metric.
  • Ablation Studies: Understanding which components contribute to performance is crucial. I design ablations to isolate the impact of each architectural choice.
  • Visualization & Interpretation: Results are communicated not just as numbers but through visualizations that provide intuition about model behavior.
  • Practical Implementation: Theory is validated through implementation, ensuring feasibility in real-world scenarios.
  Ongoing Learning Areas

As a researcher, I am committed to continuous learning and exploration:

  • Vision-Language Models: Exploring the intersection of vision and language understanding for more comprehensive AI systems.
  • Generative Models: Understanding and developing GANs, diffusion models, and other generative approaches.
  • Neural Architecture Search: Automating the design of neural network architectures for specific tasks.
  • Efficient AI: Developing techniques for model compression, quantization, and efficient inference.
  • Causal Inference: Understanding causal relationships in data beyond correlation-based learning.
  • Scientific Computing: Applying deep learning to scientific discovery and simulation.
